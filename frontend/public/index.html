How the café works

If Kubernetes ran a café...

Every intimidating Kubernetes term becomes something you've already seen in daily life.

01
🚪
INGRESS

The front door

Every customer enters through one door. The host decides whether they need the dining area or the kitchen API.

/ → frontend    /api → backend
02
🛎️
SERVICE

The waiter

Customers don't need to know which cook is free. The waiter always knows where to send the order.

03
👨‍🍳
POD

A café worker

A Pod is one running worker. If one disappears, Kubernetes can bring in another.

04
🧑‍💼
DEPLOYMENT

The shift manager

The manager decides how many workers should exist and replaces anyone who stops working.

05
📈
HPA

Rush-hour staffing

When the café gets crowded, more workers are automatically called in. When things calm down, they go home.

06
🗄️
PVC + PV

The pantry

Workers can change, but ingredients stay in storage. Persistent data survives even when Pods are replaced.

07
📋
CONFIGMAP

The notice board

Opening hours and instructions belong on the notice board, not permanently written into every worker's brain.

08
🔐
SECRET

The locked drawer

Passwords and credentials stay somewhere more appropriate than ordinary configuration.
